A STORY WRITTEN WITH LOVE · 01 / 08
A letter that took a lifetime to write
✿

My Dearest
Sarauniya.

Tonight, I don't want to just tell you that I love you.

I want to take you somewhere. Somewhere made entirely of the things my heart has been trying to tell you.

M

A little letter, just for you.

Chapter one · A beautiful coincidence

Out of all the people
in this world...

Allah allowed our paths to cross.

And somewhere between the conversations, the laughter, the little moments and the things we cannot explain, you became someone I never want to take for granted.

You became my favourite person to pray for.

Chapter two · What I see in you

Five beautiful things.

Things I hope you never stop seeing in yourself.

YOUR HEART

Your kindness, your softness, and the way you care.

YOUR STRENGTH

Even on days when you don't feel strong, you keep going.

YOUR MIND

Your thoughts, your dreams, and the person you're becoming.

YOUR FAITH

The beauty of wanting Allah at the centre of your life.

YOUR LOVE

The little ways you make me feel seen, supported and cared for.

Chapter three · Behind my dreams

There is a picture
behind everything.

Sometimes you see me working, planning, learning, trying to become better.

But I wish you could see the picture behind all of it.

I imagine a future where you don't have to wonder whether I am serious about us. Where my actions have answered every question.

A future where we look back at these early days and smile because we kept believing, kept growing and kept trusting Allah.

I am building with tomorrow in my heart.

Chapter four · The little life I dream about

A home.
Not just a house.

Imagine Fajr together. A peaceful home. You cooking, me joining you in the kitchen. Little jokes, random laughter, and meals that somehow taste better because we're sharing them.

Prayers before plans. Forgiveness after misunderstandings. Supporting each other's dreams. Growing older without growing distant.

A home where Allah is remembered and love is felt.

Chapter five · My promise
♡

I choose to keep
showing up.

I cannot promise that every day will be easy.

But I can promise to keep learning how to love you better. To communicate. To listen. To apologise when I'm wrong. To support you and to never treat your heart carelessly.

I will keep working towards the life we dream about. I will never stop loving you, and I will never give up on us.

Not just in words. In the way I live.

Chapter six · What I ask Allah for

Ya Allah...

If this love is good for our dunya and akhirah, bless it, protect it and allow it to grow in a way that pleases You.

Make our Nikkah easy and beautiful. Remove the obstacles from our path. Grant us a home filled with sakinah, rahmah and barakah.

Make us garments for one another. Help us be patient in difficulty, grateful in ease, gentle in disagreement and faithful in every season.

Let our love bring us closer to You, never further away.

Ameen ya Rabb. 🤲

The last page · Never the last chapter

To my Sarauniya,

My love, if there is one thing I want you to take away from this little experience, it is this: you matter to me more than I can ever fit into a screen.

I see you. I see your effort, your heart, your dreams, your imperfections and your beautiful ways. I don't only love the happy moments. I want to be someone who stands beside you through the ordinary days too.

When I think about my future, I don't imagine success as something I enjoy alone. I imagine us celebrating the little victories, making dua when things are difficult, and thanking Allah when He makes a way.

I pray that the day we make our Nikkah, we remember these days with grateful hearts. May Allah make everything smooth for us, and may the life ahead be even more beautiful than the one we've imagined.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for loving me. And thank you for giving my heart another reason to look forward to tomorrow.

With all the love in my heart,
Muhammad.

My Queen. My Hayati. 💗

The story continues, Insha'Allah.
